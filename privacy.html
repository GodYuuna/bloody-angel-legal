Bloody Angel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bloody Angel ("Bot"), developed by GodYuuna_S2, may collect, process, use, and store information when the Bot is used on Discord.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled on a Discord server, Bloody Angel may process the following types of information:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Server IDs
  • Channel IDs and channel configuration
  • Role IDs and role information
  • Message-related information when required for a feature
  • Moderation-related information
  • Discord audit-log information when required for moderation logging
  • XP and leveling information
  • Giveaway-related information
  • Counting-related information
  • Bot configuration and server settings

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to provide and operate Bloody Angel's features, including:

  • Moderation and server management
  • Moderation logging
  • Role-change logging
  • Lock and unlock logging
  • XP and leveling systems
  • Giveaways
  • Counting functionality
  • Welcome messages
  • Server configuration
  • Other requested Bot functionality

3. Message Information

Certain features may require Bloody Angel to process message-related information. For example, moderation logging, counting, or other features may require access to messages or message metadata.

Message-related information is only processed where necessary for the relevant Bot functionality.

4. Moderation and Audit Information

Bloody Angel may process moderation-related information such as deleted or edited messages, role changes, and other server-management events when those events are configured to be logged.

When possible, Discord Audit Logs may be used to determine the user responsible for certain administrative or role-related actions.

5. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored for as long as necessary to provide the relevant Bot functionality, maintain configuration, operate logging features, or comply with applicable obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature using it.

6. Discord API

Bloody Angel uses Discord's API and services to operate. Information processed through the Bot may therefore be subject to Discord's own systems and policies.

You can review Discord's Privacy Policy for additional information about Discord's processing of personal data.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed through Discord and other technical services required to operate Bloody Angel. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or the developer.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by Bloody Angel. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe Bloody Angel stores information associated with your Discord account and you would like to request its deletion, you may contact us by email.

Email: bloodyangelssupport@gmail.com

Please include enough information to identify the relevant Discord account or server and explain what information you would like deleted.

10. Server Administrator Responsibilities

Server owners and administrators are responsible for deciding whether Bloody Angel is appropriate for their server and for configuring the Bot and its permissions appropriately.

11. Children's Privacy

Bloody Angel is operated through Discord and does not intentionally request personal information directly from children.

Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own age requirements and policies.

12. International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure and services used to operate Bloody Angel, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

13. Third-Party Services

Bloody Angel may depend on third-party services, including Discord, hosting providers, databases, or other technical infrastructure. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this website together with a new "Last updated" date.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, data processing, or data deletion requests, please contact:

Email: bloodyangelssupport@gmail.com
